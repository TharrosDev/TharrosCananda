# Tharros Canada direction contract

FORM seed key: `86f14bb5`

## THESIS

A serious Canada–Europe commercial research company presented as an evidence ledger: small enough to be explicit about what exists, rigorous enough that every public data point can be traced, and commercial enough that the visitor always understands what can be commissioned.

## OWN-WORLD

The visual world comes from research artifacts: customs tables, procurement registers, report typography, public datasets and source annotations. Warm ivory carries deep ink; muted red marks action; steel carries quantitative series. Source Serif speaks editorially and Schibsted Grotesk operates the interface. No generic consultancy imagery or invented proof.

## STORY

The visitor begins with the commercial decision, sees the four areas of expertise, inspects a real official-data interface and source methodology, then either reads verified Tharros research when it exists or commissions a focused answer. An empty research archive is honest infrastructure, not a weakness to hide with dummy publications.

## FIRST VIEWPORT

The homepage keeps the strongest dark hero. Task pages use compact dark heroes. Expertise, archive, About, Methodology, How It Works and policy pages use light document headers to prevent every route from feeling like another landing page.

## FORM

Primary navigation is Services · Expertise · Research · Market Data · About, with Commission research as the one filled action. The Market Data interface uses official Statistics Canada values and fails closed when unavailable. Research archive controls appear automatically once real publications exist. Dense information remains ruled and flat rather than boxed into cards.
